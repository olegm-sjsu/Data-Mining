1. Motivation

Why Sound Breaks Standard Deep Learning Assumptions

General-purpose audio requires both local transient precision and global harmonic context.

The Label Bottleneck in Audio

  • AudioSet spans 2M clips / 527 classes, yet real sound mixes speech, music, and noise simultaneously.
  • Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) invents pretext tasks from raw audio without manual tags.
  • Early speech SSL (wav2vec 2.0, HuBERT) focuses on short 1D windows — strong for phonemes, weak on music & scenes.

The Transformer Scaling Tax

  • Scaled Dot-Product Attention (SDPA) costs O(N²) time & memory with sequence length N.
  • Storing the full Key-Value (KV) cache bloats memory on long clips.
  • Global attention mixes every patch equally, diluting local cues like drum hits or consonant bursts.

The Core Research Question (Yadav et al., 2025)

  • Can linear-time recurrent architectures — Mamba (Selective SSMs) and xLSTM (Extended LSTM) — outperform Transformers in Masked Spectrogram Modeling?
  • And crucially: how do we compare them fairly without Transformer-specific shortcuts?

3. MSM Mechanics

Masked Spectrogram Modeling: Sound as a 2D Puzzle

Why audio masks 50–80% of patches (vs. BERT's 15%) and how patch geometry shapes representations.

1. Patchifying Sound

  • Convert 16 kHz audio into log-mel spectrogram (2s clip → 200 frames × 80 bins).
  • Slice into non-overlapping t × f patches to prevent edge leakage.
  • Square (16×16): neutral time-freq.
  • Rectangular (4×16, default): sharper time resolution (250 patches/2s).
  • Frame-wise (1×F): max time precision for keyword spotting.

2. High Masking Ratios

  • Text is dense → BERT masks 15%.
  • Spectrograms are spatially redundant across adjacent bins & frames.
  • Masking 15% allows trivial local interpolation.
  • MSMs mask 50% (SSAST/SSAM) up to 75–80% (BEATs, AudioMAE, Dasheng).
  • Dasheng (2024) uses grouped 80ms masking to stop STFT window overlap leakage.

3. Reconstruction Loss

  • Encoder sees partial view; decoder predicts missing patches.
  • MSE computed only on masked patches outperforms full-spectrogram MSE.
  • Decoder is discarded after pretraining.
  • Variants add InfoNCE contrastive loss (orig. SSAST) or discrete codebook targets (BEATs).

6. Gist & Intuition

How Mamba and xLSTM Fix Classical Recurrence

Skip the heavy equations — here is the engineering intuition behind both challengers.

Mamba (S6) → SSAM: Content-Aware State Spaces

  • Earlier S4 models used fixed matrices — unable to dynamically select relevant context.
  • Mamba makes parameters Δ, B, C functions of the current input patch.
  • Δ acts as an adaptive focus/forget knob: large Δ resets state for a new event; small Δ preserves context across silence.
  • Hardware-aware parallel scan executes inside fast GPU SRAM.
  • Result: O(N) scaling, zero KV cache, strong noise resilience.

xLSTM (mLSTM) → AxLSTM: Matrix Key-Value Memory

  • Classical LSTMs suffered from scalar memory bottlenecks, squashing sigmoids, and sequential hidden-hidden mixing.
  • Exponential gating + log stabilizer lets xLSTM decisively revise stored values.
  • mLSTM upgrades the scalar cell to a d × d matrix memory via key-value outer products.
  • Removing hidden-hidden mixing lets ViL-style mLSTM train in parallel.
  • Result: High associative capacity suited for music & harmonic pitch.

7. Benchmark Design

Experimental Setup: Fair, Depth-Matched, and Reproducible

AudioSet pretraining + 10-task HEAR benchmark evaluation protocol (Yadav et al., 2025).

Unified Pretraining Controls

  • Dataset: AudioSet (~2M clips, >5,000 hrs), 2s random crops, no augmentations.
  • Patches: 4×16 (250 patches), 50% unstructured random mask, masked-patch MSE.
  • Depth Control: l = 12 blocks across Tiny/Small/Base; Ef = 3 for Mamba & xLSTM (fewer params than SSAST).
  • Unidirectional only: Bi-directional scans did NOT improve performance.

HEAR Evaluation & Normalized Metric s(m)

  • Frozen representations: mean-pooled over 2s chunks → 1-hidden-layer MLP (1,024 neurons), 10 runs + 95% CIs.
  • Measures pure out-of-the-box representation quality without parameter-heavy fine-tuning.
  • Aggregated Score s(m) ∈ [0, 100] averages min-max normalized scores across all 10 tasks.

The 10 Diverse HEAR Tasks (Table I)

  • Music & Pitch (4): Beijing Opera (BO), Mridangam Stroke (Mri-S), Mridangam Tonic (Mri-T), NSynth Pitch 5h (NS-5h, 88 cls).
  • Speech (4): Crema-D Emotion (CD), LibriCount Speakers (LC), SpeechCommands 5h (SC-5h), VoxLingua107 ID (VL).
  • General Audio (2): ESC-50 Environmental Sound (E50, 50 cls), FSD50K Multilabel Tagging (F50K, 200 cls, 100 hrs).

12. Beyond Backbones

Cross-Survey Synthesis: Pretraining Targets Matter Too

Integrating Mousavi et al. (2025), Dinkel et al. (2024 Dasheng), and Su et al. (2025 Audio-LLMs).

Continuous Patch MSE

  • Models: AudioMAE, MSM-MAE, SSAM, AxLSTM, Dasheng.
  • Pros: Zero tokenizer overhead; scales to 1.2B params & 272k hrs (Dasheng).
  • Cons: May spend capacity on background noise.

Hybrid Contrastive + MSE

  • Models: Original SSAST (2022), MAE-AST.
  • Pros: Adds discriminative InfoNCE signal alongside reconstruction.
  • Cons: Extra negative-sampling & loss weighting tuning.

Discrete Codebook Tokens

  • Models: BEATs, HuBERT (Mousavi et al., 2025).
  • Pros: Discards low-level noise; strong semantics (BEATs = 85.9).
  • Cons: Iterative multi-stage tokenizer training overhead.

The Unexplored Opportunity for Tier-2 Audio-LLMs (Su et al., 2025)

  • Most Audio-LLMs plug in Whisper or Transformer-MAE encoders. Pairing linear-time Mamba/xLSTM encoders with BEATs-style semantic targets could dramatically cut compute on long audio streams.

13. My Two Cents

Critical Reflections: What the Benchmarks Don't Shout

Four grounded takeaways and caveats from reading across the audio SSL literature.

1. Frozen Probing ≠ Fine-Tuning

  • HEAR tests frozen 2s mean-pooled embeddings + 1-layer MLP. That exposes out-of-the-box features, yet severely penalizes AudioMAE (63.1 frozen vs. strong end-to-end fine-tuning).

2. MAE's 4× Compute Lock-In

  • MAE Transformers drop 75–80% of tokens during pretraining. Mamba & xLSTM cannot drop patches without breaking sequence order—keeping MAE Transformers cheaper at 1B+ scale for now.

3. Don't Blindly Copy Vision Bi-Dir

  • Unlike Vision Mamba/ViL, Yadav et al. found bidirectional scans did NOT improve audio MSMs. Spectrograms have a causal time arrow; reverse passes can waste compute.

4. The Endgame Is Hybrid Local-Global

  • Top Base models are MWMAE-Base (89.4, local-global windows) and SSAM-Base (87.9, selective SSM). Combining SSM sweeps with windowed attention + Tiled Flash kernels is the clearest path forward.

14. Summary

Practitioner Decision Guide & Key References

Choosing the right Tier-1 audio representation architecture for your constraints.

Quick Selection Matrix

  • Edge / Tiny Budget (<20M params, speech & general audio): Mamba SSAM-Tiny/Small (75.0–82.8 s(m), O(N), keep t ≤ 4 frames).
  • Music, Pitch & Harmonic Tasks: xLSTM AxLSTM (71.4 on NSynth Pitch; verify memory kernels past 5s).
  • Max Accuracy + Fast Pretraining at Scale: MAE-style Local-Global Attention (MWMAE-Base 89.4 / Large 92.6) or grouped-mask Dasheng.
